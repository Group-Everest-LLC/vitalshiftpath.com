Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit vitalshiftpath.com (the “Site”), how it is used, and the choices available to you. By using the Site you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we do not collect

The self-assessment quiz on this Site is processed entirely in your browser. Your answers are not transmitted to us, not stored on any server, and not associated with you or your device. They exist only in your browser's memory and are discarded when you close or reload the page. We do not ask for your name, email address or any health information in order to display your result.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and this Site is not directed at children. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share health information with advertisers.

2. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics or advertising partners may automatically receive standard technical data when you visit, such as:

  • IP address (often truncated or anonymised), approximate region derived from it
  • Browser type, operating system, device type and screen size
  • Referring URL, pages viewed, time of visit and time on page
  • Anonymous identifiers stored in cookies or similar technologies

This data is used to keep the Site secure, to understand aggregate traffic, and to measure the performance of advertising campaigns. It is not used to identify you personally.

3. Information you give us voluntarily

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to respond to you and to keep a record of the correspondence. We do not add you to a marketing list without your explicit request.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device. This Site and its partners may use:

  • Essential cookies — required for the Site to display and function correctly.
  • Analytics cookies — to count visits and understand which content is read.
  • Advertising cookies — placed by advertising networks and affiliate programs to measure clicks, attribute referrals and limit repeated ad delivery.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking cookies may affect how parts of the Site behave. Most advertising networks also offer opt-outs through optout.aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu.

5. Third parties

This Site contains links to third-party websites, including advertisers and merchants we have an affiliate relationship with. When you follow such a link you leave our Site, and the destination site's own privacy policy and terms apply. We do not control, and are not responsible for, the data practices of those websites. We encourage you to read their policies before providing them with any information.

Our advertising and traffic partners may include content-recommendation networks, affiliate networks and analytics providers. These partners may set their own cookies as described above.

6. Data retention and security

We retain email correspondence only for as long as necessary to handle your enquiry and to meet legal or record-keeping obligations. Technical logs held by our hosting and advertising partners are retained according to their own schedules. The Site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, to request its correction or deletion, to object to or restrict processing, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Residents of California (CCPA/CPRA) and the European Economic Area or United Kingdom (GDPR) have these rights explicitly. We do not sell personal information.

To make a request, email support@vitalshiftpath.com with the subject line “Privacy request”. We may need to verify your identity before acting. We respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

8. Do Not Track

Some browsers transmit a “Do Not Track” signal. There is no common industry standard for interpreting it, and this Site does not currently respond to such signals.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@vitalshiftpath.com, or write to us at the postal address in the footer.
